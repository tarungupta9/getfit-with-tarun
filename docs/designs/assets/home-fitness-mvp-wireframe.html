Your four-week plan

Three balanced 30-minute sessions

Full-body practice on non-consecutive days, with walking or easy movement between sessions.

Monday Workout A · 30 min
Wednesday Workout B · 30 min
Friday Workout A · 30 min

Why this fits you

  • Three days leave recovery time between beginner strength sessions.
  • Thirty minutes fits a warm-up, five movement patterns, and a short cool-down.
  • Week 1 starts below maximum effort; weeks 2–4 add reps or control, not random exercises.
